Interoperability enables researchers to work across data platforms, tools, and institutions with fewer technical barriers. BDC collaborates with NIH and international partners to adopt and advance standards that make biomedical data more connected, accessible, and reusable.

## GA4GH

The [Global Alliance for Genomics and Health (GA4GH)](https://www.ga4gh.org/) develops international standards and frameworks that enable secure, responsible sharing and analysis of genomic and health data across research platforms. As a GA4GH Driver Project, BioData Catalyst helps evaluate, implement, and advance these standards in real-world research environments. By aligning with GA4GH, BDC supports greater interoperability with other NIH platforms and international research initiatives, making it easier for researchers to work with data, tools, and workflows across a growing federated research ecosystem.

## NCPI

The [NIH Cloud Platform Interoperability Effort (NCPI)](https://datascience.nih.gov/nih-cloud-platform-interoperability-effort) establishes and implements guidelines and technical standards to empower end-user analyses across participating platforms and facilitate the realization of a trans-NIH, federated data ecosystem. BDC is participating in the NCPI \- along with AnVIL, the Cancer Research Data Commons, and the Kids First Data Resource Center \- to enable cross-platform data sharing and analysis.
