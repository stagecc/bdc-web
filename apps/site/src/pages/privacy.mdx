The [National Heart, Lung, and Blood Institute](https://www.nhlbi.nih.gov/) (NHLBI) operates NHLBI BioData Catalyst® (BDC), a cloud-based ecosystem that serves as an [NIH controlled-access data repository (CADR)](https://grants.nih.gov/policy-and-compliance/policy-topics/sharing-policies/accessing-data/requirements). BDC consists of an ecosystem that provides a secure cloud environment where authorized researchers can find, access, share, store, and analyze research data.

Protecting your privacy is important to us. This policy explains what information may be collected when you visit the BDC website or interact with BDC services and how that information is used and protected.

This policy applies to the public BDC website and, where applicable, services accessed through it. Some BDC platforms, identity providers, and third-party services maintain separate privacy policies, terms of use, data-use requirements, or government system-use notices. Those requirements also apply when you use those services.

## Information Collected Automatically

When you browse the BDC website, certain information about your visit may be collected automatically. This information may include:

* The Internet Protocol address or domain from which you access the internet  
* The date and time of your visit  
* The pages, documents, and links you view  
* The website that referred you to the BDC website  
* Information about your browser, operating system, and device  
* Information about how you navigate and interact with the website

BDC uses this information to understand how visitors use the website, improve its content and usability, maintain website performance, and protect the website and its users. This information is not collected for commercial marketing.

## Information You Choose to Provide

You do not have to provide personal information to browse the public BDC website.

BDC may collect personally identifiable information when you choose to provide it, such as when you:

* Submit a Contact or Get Help form  
* Send BDC an email  
* Register for an event, training, or other BDC activity  
* Provide website feedback  
* Request information, assistance, or access to a BDC service

Depending on the request, this information may include your name, email address, institution, professional role, and the contents of your message.

BDC uses the information you provide to respond to your request, provide support or requested services, communicate with you about your inquiry, and improve BDC services. Access is limited to authorized federal personnel, contractors, and service providers who require the information to perform official duties.

BDC does not sell personal information or use it for commercial marketing. Information may be disclosed when necessary to perform authorized government functions or when required by federal law, regulation, court order, or an authorized law-enforcement investigation.

Do not submit controlled-access research data, participant-level data, protected health information, credentials, or other sensitive information through public website forms or unencrypted email. BDC will specifically instruct you to use an approved method.

## Accounts and Authenticated BDC Services

Some BDC services require users to sign in through an approved identity provider and may require authorization to access controlled data.

To administer these services, BDC systems may collect and maintain information such as:

* Your name, email address, institutional affiliation, and account identifiers  
* Information used to verify your identity or research affiliation  
* Data-access permissions and authorization status  
* Account, system-usage, and support information  
* Security, audit, and activity logs

This information may be used to authenticate users, verify data-access permissions, provide and support BDC services, protect system security, meet federal oversight requirements, and enforce applicable terms of use and data-use requirements.

Authenticated systems may display additional U.S. Government system-use warnings or privacy notices. Those notices apply to activity conducted within those systems.

## Research Data and Participant Privacy

BDC hosts controlled-access data. The collection, submission, access, and use of those data are governed by applicable laws, NIH and NHLBI policies, informed-consent provisions, data-use limitations, institutional certifications, data-access agreements, and other study- or dataset-specific requirements.

This website privacy policy describes information collected from visitors and users of BDC services. It does not replace or modify the requirements governing research data, the privacy protections established for research participants, or a user’s responsibilities under an approved data-access request or agreement.

BDC applies security controls aligned with the NIST Special Publication 800-53 and applicable NIH security requirements for controlled-access data repositories.

## Cookies and Website Analytics

Cookies are small files that a website may place on your device to support website functions or remember information about a browsing session.

BDC may use session cookies for technical purposes, such as navigation, authentication, website functionality, and security. Session cookies generally expire when you close your browser.

BDC may also use federally approved web-measurement technologies to understand aggregate website use and improve the website. These technologies are not used to collect personal information for commercial marketing.

You may configure your browser to block or remove cookies. Blocking cookies may affect the operation of certain website or platform features.

Cookies and other technologies used by linked BDC platforms, identity providers, embedded services, or third-party websites are governed by the privacy policies applicable to those services.

## Links to Other Websites and Services

The BDC website links to other federal websites, BDC platforms, documentation services, identity providers, research resources, and websites operated by non-governmental organizations or private companies.

When you follow a link to another website or service, you are subject to that website’s privacy and security policies. BDC does not control how external websites collect, use, or protect information.

## Website and System Security

BDC uses administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect its website, systems, and information. 

For security purposes and to ensure that BDC services remain available to authorized users, software programs may monitor network and system traffic to identify unauthorized attempts to access, upload, alter, remove, or damage information or otherwise interfere with BDC services.

Information obtained through security monitoring may be used to investigate suspected security incidents and may be provided to authorized officials or law-enforcement personnel as required by law and applicable legal processes.

The security protections applicable to BDC systems do not extend to a visitor’s personal device, institutional network, or an external website or service linked from BDC.

## Information Retention

BDC retains personal information only for as long as necessary to respond to a request, provide or support a service, meet security and operational requirements, or comply with applicable federal records-retention requirements.

Information maintained as part of a federal system of records is protected and managed in accordance with the Privacy Act of 1974 and other applicable requirements.

## Updates to This Policy

BDC may update this policy to reflect changes to the website, BDC services, federal requirements, or privacy and security practices. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised “Last updated” date.
