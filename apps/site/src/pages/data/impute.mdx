The TOPMed Imputation Server is a specialized BDC-supported service powered by software invented and developed by the [University of Michigan](https://imputationserver.sph.umich.edu/index.html#!) and driven by data provided by the investigators of the [TOPMed Program](https://nhlbiwgs.org/).

## What is genotype imputation?

Genotype imputation is a statistical method used in genetic association studies to infer genetic variants that were not directly measured in a study’s samples. It works by comparing short segments of an individual’s genome with previously characterized genomes in a reference panel.

Imputation can expand an array-genotyped dataset from hundreds of thousands of directly measured variants to tens of millions of high-confidence variants. This additional information can increase statistical power, support meta-analysis across studies, and help researchers interpret association signals.

The TOPMed Imputation Server allows registered users to perform genotype imputation for samples they provide.

## Why use the TOPMed Imputation Server?

* **Large and diverse reference panel**  
  Use the TOPMed reference panel to improve the coverage and accuracy of genotype imputation across diverse study populations.  
* **Automated validation and quality control**  
  Uploaded files are validated before processing, and variants that do not meet quality-control requirements are identified and filtered.  
* **Integrated phasing and imputation**  
  The server can phase unphased genotype data and perform imputation through an automated workflow using established tools and methods.  
* **Secure, no-cost service**  
  Researchers can register and use the service without cost. Uploaded files and completed results are protected through access controls and encryption.

## How it works

1. **Prepare and upload genotype files**  
   Prepare phased or unphased GWAS genotype data that meet the server’s file and quality requirements, then upload the files through the secure service.  
2. **Configure the imputation job**  
   Select the appropriate genome build, phasing option, quality settings, and other job parameters.  
3. **Run quality control, phasing, and imputation**  
   The server validates the uploaded files, performs quality control, phases the data when needed, and runs genotype imputation.  
4. **Download encrypted results**  
   When the job is complete, the server sends an email notification. Results are compressed and encrypted, and a one-time password is provided separately. Completed-job data must be downloaded within seven days.

## Before you begin

* Registration and email verification are required.  
* Users submit genotype data from their own study samples.  
* Files must meet the format and quality requirements described in the technical documentation.  
* The server currently supports hg19 and hg38 input data.  
* The TOPMed reference panel uses hg38 coordinates; hg19 positions are converted during processing.  
* Phased and unphased genotype files are supported.  
* Completed results are available for seven calendar days and cannot be recovered after deletion.

## Security and privacy

Uploaded data and results are protected through access controls and encryption. Users can retrieve only results associated with their own accounts.

Following successful imputation, results are compressed and encrypted. The decryption password is sent by email and is not stored by the server. All uploaded data and completed-job results are automatically deleted after seven days.
