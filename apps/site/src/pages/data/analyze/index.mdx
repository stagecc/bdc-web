# Why Analyze Data in BDC?

**Work with data in the cloud**  
Analyze eligible data without downloading and maintaining separate local copies. You do not pay for storage of BDC-hosted data. 

**Scale computing to your research**  
Select computing resources based on the size and complexity of your analysis and adjust them as your research needs change.

**Use familiar tools and workflows**  
Work in interactive environments, use existing applications and reusable workflows, or bring your own tools.

**Collaborate securely**  
Organize data, tools, and results in a controlled environment where you can work with authorized collaborators.

# What Data Can You Analyze?

## BDC-Hosted Data

Open-access and controlled-access BDC-hosted datasets can be found through BDC’s data exploration tools. Depending on the dataset and your access approvals, you will be able to select files, build a cohort, and connect data to an analysis environment.

<Link to="/data/explore">Explore Data in BDC</Link>

## Data You Bring to BDC

Upload or connect data that you or your research team are authorized to use. You can analyze this data independently or combine it with other eligible data when permitted.

Data you bring to BDC is different from consortium data that has already been made available through a designated BDC-Seven Bridges project.

<Link to="/data/share">Learn About Bringing Data to BDC in our documentation</Link>

## Consortium Data in BDC-Seven Bridges

Some participating research consortia and programs make data available to authorized members through designated BDC-Seven Bridges projects. Researchers should follow the access instructions provided by their consortium or program.

If your Research Community is interested in hosting data in BDC, <Link to="/help/contact-bdc">contact us</Link>.

# Choose an Analysis Platform

BDC has two analysis environments, BDC-Seven Bridges and BDC-Terra, which are secure cloud spaces where researchers organize data, run analyses, and collaborate. In BDC-Seven Bridges, this environment is called a project. In BDC-Terra, it is called a workspace.

## Start with BDC-Seven Bridges

BDC-Seven Bridges supports a broad range of BDC research needs and experience levels. Researchers can work with BDC-hosted data, access authorized consortium data, use graphical and code-based tools, run reusable workflows, and collaborate in secure projects.

BDC-Seven Bridges is generally the best place to begin when you:

* Are new to analyzing data in BDC  
* Need to access data provided through a participating consortium or research program  
* Want both graphical and code-based analysis options  
* Need preconfigured applications, workflows, or interactive environments  
* Want to build or bring your own tools and workflows

BDC-Seven Bridges is the only BDC analysis platform where authorized researchers can access consortium data.

## When BDC-Terra May Be a Better Fit

BDC-Terra provides a Google Cloud-based environment for researchers whose work is already connected to Terra or the broader Terra ecosystem.

Consider BDC-Terra when you:

* Already use Terra or collaborate with researchers who do  
* Need to work with eligible BDC data alongside resources available through AnVIL or another Terra-based program  
* Specifically need a Terra workspace for your research workflow

## Compare BDC Analysis Platforms

| Consideration | BDC-Seven Bridges | BDC-Terra |
| :---- | :---- | :---- |
| **Best starting point for** | Most BDC analysis needs | Existing Terra-centered workflows |
| **Consortium data** | Available to authorized members | Not available |
| **Interactive analysis** | JupyterLab, RStudio, SAS (hourly license fees apply), and specialized environments | Jupyter, RStudio, and Terra-supported environments |
| **Workflow emphasis** | Prebuilt and custom workflows, including graphical workflow tools | Primarily WDL-based workflows |
| **Cloud environment** | AWS or Google Cloud Platform; Azure upon request | Google Cloud |

## How Analyzing Data in BDC Works

### 1\. Identify and Access Your Data

Determine whether you will use BDC-hosted data, data you bring to BDC, consortium data available through BDC-Seven Bridges, or an approved combination.

Controlled-access data requires the appropriate data access approvals; you can check if you have access to data [here](/data/explore/#requirements-for-accessing-bdc-hosted-data-2). Consortium data requires authorization through the participating consortium or program.

### 2\. Choose an Analysis Platform

Start with BDC-Seven Bridges unless your existing workflow, collaborators, or program connections make BDC-Terra a better fit.

Researchers working with consortium data must use BDC-Seven Bridges.

### 3\. Create or Access an Analysis Environment

Create a project in BDC-Seven Bridges or a workspace in BDC-Terra.

Researchers working with consortium data may instead be invited to an existing BDC-Seven Bridges project created for their consortium or program.

An approved billing account, billing group, or other funding source is required to run analyses. New users should consider [requesting cloud credits](/help/costs#cloud-credit-request-form-2).

### 4\. Connect Your Data and Begin Analysis

Connect to data in your project or workspace and then use interactive environments, applications, workflows, or your own tools to conduct your analysis and collaborate with your research team.
