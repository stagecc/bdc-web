## About Data in BDC

BDC provides secure access to a diverse and growing collection of biomedical research data focused on heart, lung, blood, and sleep health. The ecosystem hosts and integrates large-scale clinical, genomic, imaging, multi-omics, phenotypic, behavioral, environmental, and emerging digital health datasets, enabling researchers to discover, access, analyze, and share data through cloud-based platforms and tools. Anyone can freely explore the data that BDC hosts without logging in or requesting permission, and there is no cost for exploring data in BDC.

To see details about the hosted data in BDC, refer to the [Release Notes](https://bdcatalyst.gitbook.io/biodata-catalyst-documentation/written-documentation/release-notes) section of the BDC documentation.

All three of the above searches provide Study Accession IDs - unique, stable, and   versioned identifiers.

The search capabilities of "Keywords in Study Names or Descriptions" and "Disease, Phenotype, Biological Process, or Anatomical Entity" search across the data  dictionaries to explore variables in the studies.

The "Disease, Phenotype, Biological Process, or Anatomical Entity" search also  returns biomedical concepts and study variables that are related to the search term.

The "Phenotypes" search includes participant-level data (aggregate counts returned),  variable distributions, data dictionary, and Study Accession IDs.

Refer to the [Explore Available Data](https://bdcatalyst.gitbook.io/biodata-catalyst-documentation/written-documentation/explore-available-data) section  of the BDC documentation to learn more about each of these searches.

## Assess Feasibility
  
To determine whether BDC-hosted data is sufficient to address a specific scientific question - a process known as a feasibility assessment - researchers can search by various terms, including study names, variables, phenotypes, and even Study Accession IDs (if known). After selecting “Conduct a Feasibility Assessment” below, users can enter their search criteria and then apply filters to retrieve aggregated participant counts, helping them gauge whether the available data meets their research needs.

<Button variant="secondary" centered href="https://picsure.biodatacatalyst.nhlbi.nih.gov/discover">Conduct Feasibility Assessment</Button>

## Requirements for Accessing BDC Hosted Data

Researchers who want to use **controlled-access datasets** must have an approved Data Access Request (DAR) for each dataset through the [NIH Database of Genotypes and Phenotypes (dbGaP)](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/gap/). To submit one or more DARs, refer to NIH guidance on [How to Request and Access Datasets from dbGaP](https://grants.nih.gov/policy-and-compliance/policy-topics/sharing-policies/accessing-data/dbgap).
