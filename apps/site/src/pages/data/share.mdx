Researchers can use BDC as a data repository when preparing to submit or share data in accordance with NIH and NHLBI data sharing expectations. This page is intended for researchers and study teams preparing to make data available through BDC.

*If you want to analyze BDC-hosted data or bring your own data into a workspace for analysis, visit the [Analyze Data](/data/analyze) page.*

## BDC Data Submission Instructions

The BDC Data Management Core (DMC) helps researchers submit data into BDC. BDC currently accepts a variety of data types for controlled and open access, which include clinical assessment (such as clinical observations, laboratory measurements, survey, and EHR/EMR), omics data (such as WGS, RNA-Sequencing, DNA Methylation, Metabolome, Proteome, and so on), medical imaging data (such as MRI, ECG, and CT Scan), sleep data (such as polysomnography), wearables (such as actigraphy and smartwatch). Researchers preparing to submit data should begin with the data submission instructions, which explain the submission process and help study teams understand what to prepare before working with the DMC. BDC uses the [NIH database of Genotypes and Phenotypes (dbGaP)](https://dbgap.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/home/) for registration and access.

# NIH Data Management and Sharing Policies

NIH has data-sharing policies that researchers must abide by, including the NIH [Data Management and Sharing (DMS) Policy (effective January 25, 2023\)](https://sharing.nih.gov/data-management-and-sharing-policy/about-data-management-and-sharing-policies/data-management-and-sharing-policy-overview#after). Researchers who plan to use BDC as their data repository should include "NHLBI BioData Catalyst" in their [data management and sharing plans](https://sharing.nih.gov/data-management-and-sharing-policy/planning-and-budgeting-for-data-management-and-sharing/writing-a-data-management-and-sharing-plan#after).

[→ Refer to NIH Data Management and Sharing](https://grants.nih.gov/policy-and-compliance/policy-topics/sharing-policies/dms) for more information
