Researchers can use BDC as a data repository when preparing to submit or share data in accordance with NIH and NHLBI data sharing expectations. This page is intended for researchers and study teams preparing to make data available through BDC.

*If you want to analyze BDC-hosted data or bring your own data into a workspace for analysis, visit the [Analyze Data](/data/analyze) page.*

## BDC Data Submission Instructions

Researchers preparing to submit data to BDC should begin with the data submission instructions, which explain the submission process and help study teams understand what to prepare before working with the BDC Data Management Core (DMC). BDC uses the [NIH database of Genotypes and Phenotypes (dbGaP)](https://dbgap.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/home/) for registration and access.

# NIH Data Management and Sharing Policies

NIH — including its institutes and centers — has data-sharing policies that researchers must abide by, including the NIH [Data Management and Sharing (DMS) Policy (effective January 25, 2023)](https://sharing.nih.gov/data-management-and-sharing-policy/about-data-management-and-sharing-policies/data-management-and-sharing-policy-overview#after), which provides information about [selecting a data repository](https://sharing.nih.gov/data-management-and-sharing-policy/sharing-scientific-data/selecting-a-data-repository). 

Researchers who plan to use BDC as their data repository should include "NHLBI BioData Catalyst" in their [data management and sharing plans](https://sharing.nih.gov/data-management-and-sharing-policy/planning-and-budgeting-for-data-management-and-sharing/writing-a-data-management-and-sharing-plan#after).

[→ Refer to NIH Data Management and Sharing](https://grants.nih.gov/policy-and-compliance/policy-topics/sharing-policies/dms) for more information
