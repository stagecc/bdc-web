BDC is committed to providing an accessible experience for all users. We work to ensure that the BDC website and digital content are usable by the widest possible audience, including people who use assistive technologies.

Specifically, we aim to meet the requirements of:

* [Section 508 of the Rehabilitation Act](https://www.access-board.gov/about/law/ra.html)  
* [Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA](https://www.w3.org/WAI/standards-guidelines/wcag/)

Accessibility is an ongoing effort. We regularly review BDC website content and functionality and work to identify and address accessibility barriers as they are found.

Learn more about federal digital accessibility requirements through the [U.S. Access Board](https://www.access-board.gov/ict/) or [Section508.gov](https://www.section508.gov/).

## Report an accessibility issue

We welcome feedback on the accessibility of BDC. If you encounter an accessibility barrier or need information in another accessible format, let us know by completing the [Contact Us](/help/contact-bdc/) form. If you are unable to use the form, email [biodatacatalyst@nhlbi.nih.gov](mailto:biodatacatalyst@nhlbi.nih.gov). We aim to respond within 2 \- 3 business days.

When reporting an issue, please include:

* The page URL  
* A description of the issue  
* What you were trying to do when the issue occurred  
* The assistive technology, device, browser, or operating system you are using, if applicable
* Your preferred accessible format, if you are requesting information in another format
