## Data Use Policy Statement

BDC adheres to internationally recognized policies for data access and release developed to enable broad access to data on the BDC ecosystem. Data on the BDC ecosystem are subject to general and dataset-specific data use policies, and access to controlled data is restricted to authorized users. Each BDC user is solely responsible for adhering to Data Use Agreement(s), Institutional Review Board policies and guidelines, and other Data Use Limitations when uploading or importing data or downloading analysis results on the BDC ecosystem.

Whether working with hosted or their own data, BDC users are obligated to protect participant privacy and follow NIH data protection policies. User institutions are accountable and may be subject to sanctions if policies are violated.

## Data Download Restrictions

<InlineAlert variant="warning">
**Important**: Individual-level data may not be downloaded from BDC.
</InlineAlert>

Download capabilities are limited to metadata and analysis results, and the latter must comply with restrictions defined by NIH for [Summary Results](https://grants.nih.gov/grants/guide/notice-files/NOT-OD-19-023.html). **Downloading sequences, variants, images, phenotypes, and other individual data is prohibited.** 

BDC notifies users of download restrictions at multiple points, including when users receive cloud credits, log in, and request downloads. Data egress is actively monitored and violations to the download policy will be subject to disciplinary actions on behalf of the NIH.

## Controlled Data Access Approval and Use

Access to controlled data hosted in BDC is managed through the [Database of Genotypes and Phenotypes](https://dbgap.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/home/) (dbGaP) <InfoTooltip text="NIH system used to manage access to controlled-access datasets" /> system and authorized by the appropriate Data Access Committee (DAC)<InfoTooltip text="Responsible for reviewing and approving requests to controlled-access datasets" />. DACs are established based on programmatic areas of interest as well as technical and ethical expertise. All DACs operate through common principles and under similar mechanisms to ensure the consistency and transparency of the controlled data access process. 

Approval through dbGaP allows authorized users to access approved data within BDC; it does not permit downloading individual-level data from BDC. Authorized users are responsible for following all applicable Data Use Agreements, Institutional Review Board policies and guidelines, Data Use Limitations, and NIH data protection requirements when working with BDC-hosted data.

Approved access is connected to a researcher’s NIH electronic Research Administration (eRA) identity through NIH Researcher Auth Service (RAS) authentication and authorization. This helps ensure that only data approved for a researcher are available to that user in BDC. RAS facilitates access to data repositories across multiple NIH-funded data platforms for researchers internal and external to NIH. RAS also provides account identity consolidation so researchers can move from system to system using one set of credentials. For more information about RAS, refer to [NIH's Registration Guidance page](https://auth.nih.gov/CertAuthV3/forms/IDPregistration.html). 

Individual-level data currently hosted within BDC is de-identified. De-identified data is information that has been stripped of direct and indirect identifiers, reducing the risk that data can be linked to a specific person.

## Citing and Acknowledging BDC

Researchers who use BDC resources, services, tools, funding, or cloud credits are expected to appropriately acknowledge BDC’s contribution to their work. Acknowledgement and citation should be included in publications, preprints, posters, presentations, and other scholarly outputs resulting from the use of BDC.  

Recipients of support through programs such as NHLBI BDC Pilot Funding Program or Cloud Credit Support Program are also expected to acknowledge that support in any resulting scholarly outputs. 

Standard [citation and acknowledgement language](https://bdcatalyst.gitbook.io/biodata-catalyst-documentation/community/citation-and-acknowledgement) is available in BDC documentation or can be copied from below.
