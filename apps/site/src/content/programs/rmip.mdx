BDC is supporting the development of a centralized research resource for [Regenerative Medicine Innovation Project (RMIP)](https://www.nih.gov/regenerative-medicine-innovation-project-rmip) data, with standardized data aggregation and processing across multiple data-generation sites. BDC also supports study registration and data ingest, helping make these data available for future research and reuse.
