Mark Effingham is the Deputy CEO of UK Biobank and brings broad experience in technical strategy, IT delivery, and domain expertise in health informatics and big data. He is a Chartered IT Professional and a Fellow of the British Computer Society. He holds a Ph.D. and M.Sc. from the University of Birmingham.

Dr. Effingham has been closely associated with the large-scale programs to add genotyping, exome and whole genome sequencing data to the UK Biobank resource, and has led implementation of UK Biobank’s new cloud-based Research Analysis Platform that provides additional ways for the international research community to engage with this growing petabyte-scale repository of richly curated health data.
