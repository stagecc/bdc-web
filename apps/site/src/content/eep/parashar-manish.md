Manish Parashar is the inaugural Chief AI Officer for the University of Utah. He is also the Executive Director of the Scientific Computing and Imaging Institute and Presidential Professor in the University of Utah’s Kahlert School of Computing. He recently completed an IPA appointment at the National Science Foundation as Office Director of the NSF Office of Advanced Cyberinfrastructure, where he oversaw investments in national cyberinfrastructure.
