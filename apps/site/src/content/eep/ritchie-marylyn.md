Marylyn D. Ritchie, Ph.D., FACMI, is Chief Artificial Intelligence Officer at the Medical University of South Carolina (MUSC), where she also serves as Enterprise Director of the MUSC AI Center for Health Innovation and Informatics and Associate Dean for Artificial Intelligence. A nationally recognized leader in translational bioinformatics and SmartState Endowed Chair in Translational Biomedical Informatics, Dr. Ritchie has spent more than 20 years developing innovative methods that integrate genomics and electronic health records to advance the understanding of complex diseases.
