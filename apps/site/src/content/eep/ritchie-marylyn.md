Marylyn D. Ritchie, Ph.D., FACMI, is the chief artificial intelligence officer (CAIO) for the Medical University of South Carolina (MUSC) Enterprise Director of the MUSC AI Center for Health Innovation and Informatics, as well as Associate Dean for Artificial Intelligence and Director of the Division of Computational Health Sciences and Al in the College of Medicine at the Medical University of South Carolina (MUSC). Dr. Ritchie is also the SmartState Endowed Chair in Translational Biomedical Informatics.

Dr. Ritchie is a nationally recognized leader in translational bioinformatics, with more than 20 years of experience developing methods and tools that integrate genomics with electronic health records to better understand complex disease. She has authored more than 500 publications and has been honored as an ACMI Fellow, a member of the National Academy of Medicine, and an ELAM alumna. In her current role at MUSC, she is leading the institution’s AI strategy, advancing collaborative initiatives across education, research, and patient care, while her research applies statistical, computational, machine learning, and AI approaches to connect multi-omics, clinical, environmental, and social determinants of health data for deeper insights into disease.
