Managing a research project on BDC involves more than getting started; it's also important to know how to properly onboard new team members, manage access and roles, and offboard staff and projects when circumstances change.

In this session, we'll walk through key considerations and practical steps for bringing your project and staff onto BDC, maintaining appropriate access throughout the project lifecycle, and ensuring a smooth transition when team members leave or a project comes to an end.

Perfect for Principal Investigators (PIs), lab managers, project administrators, and anyone responsible for managing BDC projects, users, or access. All BDC users are welcome to attend.
