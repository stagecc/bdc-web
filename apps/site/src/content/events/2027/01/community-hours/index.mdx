Join us for a focused session on cloud cost estimation and optimization using Spot Instances on BDC-Seven Bridges. We'll explore how to estimate compute costs, identify opportunities for savings, and make effective use of Spot Instances for suitable workloads.

The session will cover practical approaches to cost planning, Spot Instance selection, workload suitability, and potential cost savings, helping you make more informed decisions when running workloads. We will also discuss NHLBI's Cloud Credit request program.

Perfect for researchers, developers, and teams looking to optimize cloud compute spending while making the most of available infrastructure.
