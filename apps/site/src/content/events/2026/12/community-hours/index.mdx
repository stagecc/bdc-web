Join us for 20-minute tour introducing BDC-Seven Bridges for new and experienced users. We’ll provide an overview of the platform, highlighting popular tools and workflows. Get best practice tips on creating and developing projects, managing permissions, and billing groups.

New users will learn the fundamentals, how to set projects up for success, and where to find help and resources. More experienced users will gain strategies for improving collaboration and efficiency.

Even if you cannot attend the session live, you can still [register](https://renci.zoom.us/webinar/register/WN_8RlkidTbQhmNK223zE5Rlg). Materials and videos will be distributed in the BDC newsletter. Sign up to receive the newsletter [here](/join-bdc/).
